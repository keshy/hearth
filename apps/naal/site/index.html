              Speakers, TVs and Bluetooth

              Nest speakers, Chromecasts and Cast TVs: tap the device button above the songs and pick one. The home server plays the music on it, so it keeps going when this phone locks, and anyone in the house can open Naal to pause or skip.

              Bluetooth: choose “This device”; songs play through whatever this phone or laptop is connected to. Pair the speaker in the device's Bluetooth settings first.

              On “This device” the screen stays on while a song plays, because a locked phone pauses web pages. To listen here with the screen off, use the ↗ next to a song to open it in YouTube Music.
